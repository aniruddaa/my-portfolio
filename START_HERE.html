🎯Getting Started in 5 Steps

Step 1: View Your Portfolio

Open index.html in your web browser. Your complete portfolio is ready to see!

Step 2: Access Admin Panel

Click "Admin" button in navigation or go to admin/login.html. Use credentials: admin / admin123

Step 3: Customize Your Information

Edit index.html to replace demo content with your own information (name, skills, projects, etc.)

Step 4: Update Your Photo

Add your profile photo to the assets/ folder and update image paths in index.html

Step 5: Deploy Online

Upload to GitHub Pages (free), Netlify (free), or your hosting provider. Share your live portfolio!

🐛Troubleshooting

Website not loading?
Check that all files are in correct folders. Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete) and try again.
Admin panel issues?
Enable localStorage in your browser. Check browser console (F12) for errors.
Animations not showing?
Verify CDN links are loading. Try a different browser or disable extensions.
