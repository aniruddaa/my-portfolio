Key Features

Task Management

Create, assign, and organize tasks with customizable priorities, due dates, and categories. Track task status from creation to completion.

Real-time Collaboration

Multiple users can work simultaneously with instant updates, live comments, and collaborative editing features.

Notifications

Smart notifications for task assignments, updates, comments, and deadlines to keep team members informed.

Project Organization

Organize tasks into projects, boards, and sprints. Use Kanban, List, or Calendar views based on preference.

Time Tracking

Built-in time tracking to log hours spent on tasks and analyze team productivity and resource allocation.

Reports & Export

Generate comprehensive reports on task completion, team performance, and project timelines. Export to multiple formats.
